Pages & reading

Multi-page and the story editor

Cross-page edits have no single undo step. Moving or duplicating a story-editor field to another page writes two documents; undo restores the page you are ON, and the other page's half of the operation is already committed. Check the target before you move.
Switching pages stashes and re-encodes. The page you leave is saved into the work folder's bytes and the page you open is decoded — that is why a page switch can take a moment on large pages, and why undo history is per-page. There is no keep-pages-open option; every switch saves-and-closes (CSP can be configured either way).
Work Settings apply without an undo step. Story text, binding direction, print margins, expression and spine changes from Manga ▸ Work Settings write immediately — they are not in the undo history at all (CSP's own dialog warns the same about its multi-page settings).

The reader

Flags and the last-read screen persist per work folder. F flags the spread you are reading ("this hand is wrong"), the ⚑ panel holds a note per flagged page, and closing the reader writes both — plus the screen you stopped on — into the work folder. Reopen the app tomorrow and the reader resumes where you left off, flags and notes intact.
1 is the tone-moiré check. In the reader, 1 (or the 1:1 bar button) shows the page at true canvas size — one canvas pixel per screen pixel — so what you squint at is the real rasterized tone, not an upscaled preview. Drag pans; page turns keep working; 1 again returns to fit.
